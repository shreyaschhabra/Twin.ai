# TrustTwin - Runtime Only Constraint

## The Boundary

The fundamental rule of TrustTwin is the **Runtime-Only Constraint**.

The system simulates a physical factory (the `Simulation Engine`) and then layers a digital twin (the `Intelligence Layer`) on top of it. 
To ensure the digital twin is robust and realistic, it **must not** have omniscient access to the physical simulation. 

## The `PublicEvent` Stream

The only communication between the physical factory and the intelligence layer is the `PublicEvent` stream, generated by the `Observability Policy`.
The Observability Policy models real-world sensor limitations:
- **Noise**: Sensor readings may be inaccurate.
- **Dropouts**: Sensors may stop sending data.
- **Delay**: Data may not arrive instantly.
- **Maturity Levels**: Different stations have different sensor capabilities (RICH, PARTIAL, POOR), which dictates what events they can emit.

## Developer Rules

When building the FastAPI application and integrating the intelligence services:
1. **Never** read internal properties of the `SimBuffer`, `SimStation`, or `SimVehicle` objects directly.
2. **Always** route the output of `run_simulation()` through `build_public_event_stream()`.
3. **Only** pass `PublicEvent` objects or features derived *strictly* from `PublicEvent` objects into the intelligence services (Trust, Flow, Quality, Anomaly).
4. If a service needs a piece of data that is not available in the `PublicEvent` stream, that service must be redesigned to infer it, or the observability policy must be explicitly (and realistically) upgraded. Do not bypass the policy.
